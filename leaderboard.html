Signal is separate from staking.

Signal is the prediction edge (the lower bound on paired log-loss improvement against the matched market). Staking is how much is risked on that signal. Rank is set on the signal side only, so a lucky staking run cannot mask a bad signal, and a large stake never buys a higher rank. Empty cells are unavailable, not zero.
