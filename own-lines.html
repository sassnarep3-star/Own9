Method

Our model rates every team with a time-decayed ridge regression on game margins plus a home-field term (λ = 25.0, half-life 365.0 days). Each historical game is priced using only games played before it, so the back-test has no look-ahead. Home field is currently +1.66 points.

Edge = our line minus the market spread. A positive edge means our model likes the home side more than the market does. Unverified market spreads are from nflverse / nfldata - data/games.csv (Lee Sharpe's nfldata; repo snapshot d5c8272) and are not confirmed by any official source.
