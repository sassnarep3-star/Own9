Stopping and drift

Specify observation horizon, effect size, power, alpha allocation, minimum opportunity coverage, calibration tolerance, price-delay stress and maximum exposure before testing. These strategy-specific numerical limits are not yet selected or validated. Do not stop early when results look attractive. A missing feature, stale quote, failed reconciliation, changed settlement rule or unsupported market suspends the strategy. Recalibration requires a new version and a fresh untouched evaluation.
