Evidence policy

Observed values require an exact official record and independently reconciled snapshot. Proposed models and mathematical derivations are labeled as such; references provide only their stated context. Unknown observed values remain blank. League pages are not odds feeds. An HTTP 200 response is not enough: the rejected injury-policy URL returned an NFL 404 document. A known-bad URL is recorded as text on the review page rather than presented as a verified source.
