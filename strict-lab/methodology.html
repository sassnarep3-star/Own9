How the utility estimates dependence

Align both legs by exact game, player, market, threshold, forecast window and settlement convention. Never encode missing, push or void as a loss. Publish every exclusion and avoid survivor-only samples. Compute Pearson correlation on binary outcomes (phi); constant outcomes produce an undefined value, not zero. Pooling thresholds can confound the estimate, so predeclare homogeneous pair definitions or condition on the forecast.

analysis/core.py includes a deterministic 95% percentile cluster bootstrap. It resamples whole declared clusters, not individual correlated bets. The exploratory minimum of 30 clusters and 2,000 replicates are project defaults, not guarantees of coverage or statistical power. A constant resample withholds the interval instead of dropping inconvenient replicates. Dependence between clusters requires larger time blocks or a different valid method. No real-data correlation report has been run.

Implementation boundary

Implemented: settlement-aware math, score-PMF integration, two-leg feasibility checks, phi, cluster resampling, binary scoring, Holm adjustment and structural timestamp checks. Not implemented: licensed ingestion, fitted score/player model, independent semantic source reconciliation, valid hypothesis-test generation, sealed backtest, general SGP settlement engine or automated wagering. Tests use synthetic fixtures only; they are not research findings.
