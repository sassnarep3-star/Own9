Origination workflow

  1. Reconcile inputs. Preserve original official snapshots and their availability times, identifiers, revision history and permitted use.
  2. Fit only to earlier information. Learn a joint score or stat distribution from training weeks. Shrink sparse player/team effects; tune only on a later validation window.
  3. Freeze the forecast. Save code, features, thresholds, probability distribution and quote before kickoff. No retroactive feature updates.
  4. Price exact settlements. Integrate win/push/loss or all combo settlement states. Compare with actual offered payouts, not theoretical multiplied singles.
  5. Test and abstain. Walk forward by week, then evaluate the untouched final trial. Missing or inadequate evidence means no entry.
