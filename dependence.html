Every correlation\npublished with its n.

Correlations computed only from officially sourced numbers, each published with the n it was computed from, the interval the gate will or will not grant, and the verdict that decides whether a joint price may be produced at all.

The gate, not the number, decides

Every value below is computed from the sixteen official results in the feed. All four are published with n = 16, and all four fail the predeclared sample floor, so none of them may be used to price a parlay. Publishing the number with its n and then refusing to act on it is the whole point of this page.

The sixteen games in the feed are one week of one season and are not independent observations of anything a parlay would be priced on: they share a week, a rule set and a weather window. They are published because they are real and officially sourced, and they are gated because sixteen pairs cannot carry a price.

Research, not betting advice. No verified edge or guaranteed return. Blank empirical values are unavailable and unverified, never zero.
