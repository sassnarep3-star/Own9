RESEARCH DESK / VERIFIED RESULTSAs of 14 Nov 2026 · UTC

Official scores,\nnothing inferred.

A results feed built only from official NFL pages, with the settlement it supports and the settlement it does not. Sixteen games are in the feed; the feed is the input a backtest would need, and the page measures how far short of usable it still is.

What a results feed is for

A backtest needs two things joined on the same market: the outcome and the price that was available. This page is the outcome half. The price half lives in the price-snapshot store, which is empty, so the two halves do not yet meet and no return figure can be computed.
