How to use this board

Read the suggestions, then choose a path. Nothing else happens in between: no suggestion marked Awaiting decision has been implemented, and none will be until someone marks it chosen. The paths are deliberately left unranked. Ranking them would substitute this desk's preference for the owner's decision, which is the thing the section exists to prevent.

Two items are marked Proposed to the root owner rather than built, because they would edit files another session owns. They are logged as requests in COORDINATION.md instead.
