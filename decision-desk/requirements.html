All 73 requirements, grouped by area, each with its gate and its clearing number.

How to read this register

Met means the thing exists and is populated with verified data. Partial means the structure exists — a store, a gate, a register, a schema — but the verified data inside it does not yet. Not started means this session found no implementation in the repository. Rejected by design records a shortcut the requirements refuse, so it is not proposed again.

The status is this session's reading of the repository's own published pages and committed code, recorded on 2026-11-16. Where another session owns the work, the row links to its page rather than restating it. Where a status is uncertain, the gate column says so.
