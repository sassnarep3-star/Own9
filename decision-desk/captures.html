What one capture cannot do

A single dated capture is a snapshot, not a series. Three requirements — the Wednesday-to-Friday history (R54), the timestamped series (R55) and the point-in-time ledger (R47) — all need several captures per week, produced by a scheduled job. This session produced one. It is enough to prove the capture shape works and to record a hash; it is not enough to compute a delta, and no delta is published.

The previous Week 10 snapshot in the repository and this one are two observations of the same object, which makes a row-count diff possible for the first time. That diff is DD-01, and it is published as a drift rather than reconciled, because the cause is not established.
