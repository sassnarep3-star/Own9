RESEARCH DESK / PRICE & RESULTSAs of 14 Nov 2026 · UTC

The store is built.
The prices are not.
Here is the exact distance.

A price-snapshot store that records an opening and a closing number for every market originated, and the verified results feed that a backtest settles against. Both exist; one is empty because nothing has been captured, and the emptiness is measured per game, per market and per stage rather than described.

Why an empty store is the point

A store that silently filled missing prices with zero would produce a beautiful backtest and a false one. This store records the absence instead, with a reason, so 348 missing captures out of 348 expected are visible as a number. The results feed, by contrast, is full: every game in it passed its checks.

Relationship to the schema tables on Own lines: PR #37 published a field contract for the same two ideas, with `decimal_price`, `operator` and `jurisdiction` where this store uses an American price, a publisher and a provenance class. The contracts agree on the point that matters — a capture timestamp is mandatory and nothing is inferred — and they are kept separate rather than merged, because merging them would silently decide which naming a reader should trust. See F38. Both are empty for the same measured reason, and this session checked that: the two schema files in data/raw/ hold 0 data rows between them.
