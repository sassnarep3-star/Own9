RESEARCH DESK / DEMOTION GATEAs of 14 Nov 2026 · UTC

A live strategy earns its place every week.

The demotion gate sends a live strategy back to paper on predeclared, testable triggers — trailing return, edge decay, closing-line value, calibration drift or a stale feed. No strategy is live, so the gate is defined and armed but has never triggered. The leaderboard’s policy gate (a77d40e1) covers integrity and operational breaches; the quantitative thresholds here cover performance decay — both end in the same paper state.
