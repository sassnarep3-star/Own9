What this feed is · what it is not

Settled finals for 137 games (Weeks 1–9 complete, plus the one settled Week 10 game) were transcribed verbatim from the official league schedule pages on 14 Nov 2026; the per-game official game-centre URLs are in the committed feed CSV data/board/results_2026.csv. 121 of the finals also matched the committed secondary snapshot row-for-row; 0 disagreed. The secondary file has no scores for Weeks 9–10 (it is stale), so those finals rest on the official transcription alone. The feed carries results only: it contains no odds, and the league publishes none.
