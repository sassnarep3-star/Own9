# Flagged for Review

The following are unresolved findings, not proof that any wager or strategy is profitable. The earlier root dashboard was a research framework; later inherited pages also publish exploratory results that this audit has not independently verified. Leave unavailable or unconfirmed empirical values blank and label them unverified.

| NFL data collection rights | NFL.com restricts systematic retrieval to build a database without express written consent. Permission to collect, redistribute and commercialize a dataset was not established; a preliminary transcription was withdrawn. Seek appropriate rights review before automated collection or republication. | Unverified | [NFL.com terms](https://www.nfl.com/legal/terms/) |
