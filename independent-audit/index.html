Executive summary

  • The initial repository tree contained only a README. It did not contain a model, backtest, strategy leaderboard, or historic dataset.
  • We found a limited official-publisher historical snapshot, not a validated betting feed. The NFL Week 8 picks article says its FanDuel-provided lines were locked at 1:30 p.m. ET on Thursday, October 29, 2026. The article displays moneylines, one stated spread side and game totals. Matchups and final scores can be reviewed against the NFL Week 8 schedule. These are NFL.com-reported values, not proof that a user could actually have placed a wager at those odds.
  • The article is a mutable editorial page with later updates. A stated lock time is not proof of a contemporaneous, executable sportsbook quote, and the current page cannot establish exactly when all of its other content became available. Do not use later text as point-in-time features.
  • The NFL.com terms restrict systematic retrieval to compile a database without express written consent; rights to collect, republish or commercialize these data have not been established. A preliminary local transcription was withdrawn rather than published as a dataset. This is a rights concern, not a legal determination about any particular use.
  • Spread and total prices and evidence of execution are unverified and blank. There are no player-prop or parlay quotes in this snapshot. It is only one week, not a sample sufficient to validate an edge, strategy, or correlation. No simulated profit, picks, probabilities, or correlation values are claimed.
  • Luck cannot be eliminated from future outcomes. Proper out-of-sample tests, uncertainty estimates, multiple-comparison controls, and a matched benchmark can help distinguish evidence of forecasting skill from noise; they cannot guarantee profit. The American Statistical Association cautions against treating a p-value alone as the probability that a hypothesis is true or as a substitute for transparent reporting.
  • Actual reward eligibility and legal compliance are unverified. This is research, not a sponsored competition or wagering service. As one jurisdictional example, the Washington State Gambling Commission describes restrictions on sports wagering and defines gambling using prize, chance, and consideration. This example does not assert the user's location or determine the law for any particular contest.
