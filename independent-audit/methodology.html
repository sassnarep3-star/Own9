Strategy and validation handoff

Everything on this page is a proposed research protocol, not an official NFL recommendation, a claim of a measured edge, or a completed validation. No odds are represented as executable and no numerical forecasts, correlation estimates, returns, or rewards have been supplied. Definitions and methodological sources are linked; applicability to a particular market still requires testing.

Correlation protocol

Predeclare the exact two contracts, line thresholds, player/game identifiers, observation window, and inclusion rules. Align paired win/loss outcomes from the same eligible sample. Track pushes, voids, missing observations and revisions separately; do not silently code them as losses. State whether the reported association is unconditional or conditional on both legs being in action. A threshold-specific correlation should not automatically be applied to another threshold.

For binary win indicators X and Y, with nonzero variances, correlation is covariance divided by the product of standard deviations. With pX = P(X wins), pY = P(Y wins), and p11 = P(both win), this gives the following identity, not an observed NFL estimate:

rho = (p11 - pX * pY) / sqrt(pX * (1-pX) * pY * (1-pY))

If either indicator has zero variance, the coefficient is undefined; leave it blank. Record the four paired counts, cluster count, estimation window and uncertainty alongside any future result. Intervals do not erase model error or luck. A zero observed estimate is not evidence that every multi-leg combination is mutually independent. The Penn State correlation definition and independence discussion provide mathematical context. For three or more legs, pairwise relationships alone generally do not determine the full joint distribution.

Pricing and leaderboard safeguards

For a single contract where a win pays decimal odds D including returned stake, a loss loses one unit, and push or void returns the full stake, expected net return per unit is p_win * (D - 1) - p_loss, before fees. The fair decimal price under those specific assumptions is 1 + p_loss / p_win, only when p_win is positive. These are algebraic identities, not measured prices or recommendations. Partial payments, dead heats, altered prices and multi-leg settlements require their own payoff states and the applicable rules; see the operator's example rules.

Rank only strategies that pass every gate, within comparable predeclared market divisions, on the same forecast universe and timestamps. Publish paired score improvement, uncertainty and coverage together; using only voluntary selections would permit cherry-picking, and comparing raw Brier scores across different samples can be misleading according to the forecast-verification material. Keep actual rewards, prize values and ranks blank until an eligible, legally reviewed competition and sufficient independent evidence exist. These safeguards cannot eliminate luck or guarantee future profits.
