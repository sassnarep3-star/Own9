NFL own-line research

A research workspace for originating NFL lines from own models, testing them against a market baseline, and measuring correlations. The honest result so far: the simple own-line model does not beat the market, and no strategy qualifies for a leaderboard reward.

Status: not validated. No strategy passed every validation gate. Lines used for comparison come from an unverified third-party file. Nothing here is a betting recommendation; in Washington State sports wagering is restricted (see Flagged for review).
