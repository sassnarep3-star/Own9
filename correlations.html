What this means for combos

  • Pairwise dependence in this data is near zero, so independence is a reasonable baseline for cross-game parlays. The test is weak and was run on unverified lines.
  • Same-game combos (e.g. spread plus total) need a priced joint market. Only a tiny measured association exists, so none was priced.
  • Correlation alone does not price a combo. Any joint price must be validated out of sample against the actual offered price.
