Why the market column is blank

The model’s fair line is published below as a model output. The market line is blank because no official league source publishes wagering prices and the owner has not approved a book of record (open decisions S04/S05, Q28); the difference column is therefore also blank. Those cells stay blank — never zero, never guessed. The origination order is enforced by engine/board.py, which refuses to create a total market for a game whose side market does not exist, and a moneyline whose total does not exist.
