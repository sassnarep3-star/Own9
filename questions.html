RESEARCH DESK / QUESTIONSAs of 14 Nov 2026 · UTC

What has to be answered
before a decision is defensible.

Every question splits into four states: answered from an official source, answered from this repository, open and fixable with data work, or open and needing an owner decision. A question is never closed by inference, and nothing here was answered by asking you.

Current decision: research-only, no strategy eligible.

The work in this repository specifies methods and tests mathematical utilities, but does not establish NFL predictive skill, executable prices, measured player/defense effects, validated correlations, funded rewards or an automated data feed. The suggestions below are proposals for review; no model, data acquisition, wager or reward program is being silently enabled.

Q01Does the league publish an injury report at all, and in what form?Yes: a club-by-club report grouped by upcoming game with Player, Position, Injuries, Practice Status and Game Status, selectable by season and week.The report carries no publication timestamp per row.Answered · officialNFL ↗
Q02What do Questionable, Doubtful and Out actually mean?Uncertain, unlikely and will not play. Limited Participation is under 100 percent of normal repetitions; Full Participation is 100 percent.The league attaches no probability to the labels, so any play-probability number is a model output to be estimated, never a definition.Answered · officialNFL ↗
Q03Is a blank game-status cell the same as healthy?No. A player certain to play is deliberately left off the status report, and the policy instead requires an explanation if that player is later deactivated.The report alone cannot distinguish certain-to-play from unreported; the inactive list is the terminal record.Answered · officialNFL ↗ · NFL ↗
Q04How long does a reserve placement keep a player out?Under the 2022 rule change a designated player returns after four elapsed games, with eight returns permitted per club per season and at most two per player.Practice-window length, season-ending placement and playoff allocations are not in the text read.Answered · officialNFL ↗
Q05How do replacement players arrive, and how fast?Practice squads of 16, elevable to the game-day roster at most three times per player per season, plus trades, signings and waivers published as dated league transactions.Elevation counts are per player per season; a club’s remaining eligibility is not shown on the transaction rows.Answered · officialNFL ↗ · NFL ↗
Q06Does the league publish depth charts?No league depth chart was found: the club depth-chart path on the league site returns a 404 and team pages route the request to club websites.A dated negative observation of one path, not a proof of non-existence; recorded as F16.Answered · checked absenceNFL ↗
Q07Can a depth ordering be obtained anyway?Only from club sites, and the one read states its chart is unofficial, written by that site’s writers and not necessarily reflecting the organization.
