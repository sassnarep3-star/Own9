F50 · HighThe NFL game-centre and scores routes do not publish the final score as retrievable text. Both were read on 2026-11-14 and both render the score as "Hidden Score"; only the schedule by-week route and the Game Book PDFs published the number itself. Any automated results ingestion is therefore limited to those two routes, and a scrape of the game-centre page would silently produce empty scores rather than fail loudly.Checked on the official game centre for Commanders at Giants and on the Week 10 scores page; the Week 9 schedule route did publish all fifteen results as text.A dated re-check, plus a written ingestion contract that names the schedule route and the Game Book PDF as the only permitted result sources.Published finding · see Flagged for ReviewNFL ↗ · NFL ↗ · NFL ↗
F51 · HighTwo players ruled Out on the official Week 10 report appear in no column of their own club's depth chart: Omarr Norman-Lott is on neither Kansas City defensive-tackle row, and Kerby Joseph is absent from the Lions' safety rows while carrying a game status on the report. Their replacement depth cannot be derived from the published chart, so it is left blank. The Detroit safety conflict is additionally recorded as F20 by an earlier session; this row records it as it affects the depth index specifically.Official Week 10 injury report compared column by column with the Chiefs and Lions charts, both retrieved 2026-11-14.A chart or league record that lists the player, or a documented rule for handling report-chart conflicts that is applied identically to every club.Published finding · see Flagged for ReviewNFL ↗ · Kansas City Chiefs (official club site) ↗ · Detroit Lions (official club site) ↗
F52 · MediumInjury-report position labels do not resolve to depth-chart slots. The report lists "T" for Jaylon Moore and Charles Cross and "G" for Quenton Nelson, while the club charts split those into LT/RT and LG/RG. Matching had to be done by player name, which is ambiguous for players who appear in more than one slot (Anthony Bradford appears at both Seattle guard slots; Joshua Ezeudu at both Chiefs tackle-adjacent slots).Official Week 10 report positions compared with the Chiefs and Seahawks charts retrieved 2026-11-14.Shared stable player identifiers across league and club pages, or a written name-matching rule with a published conflict log.Published finding · see Flagged for ReviewNFL ↗ · Kansas City Chiefs (official club site) ↗ · Seattle Seahawks (official club site) ↗
F53 · MediumThe results feed carries no kickoff time and no overtime flag, because the schedule route publishes kickoff times only for games that have not kicked off and does not publish overtime at all. Totals cannot be settled uniformly without the overtime rule, and no time-of-day or rest comparison can be made from the feed as it stands.Both columns are present in the committed feed and empty for all sixteen rows; the absence was checked on the official schedule route, not assumed.Verify both fields from the official Game Book of each game and re-issue the feed; until then totals settlement stays blocked.Published finding · see Flagged for ReviewNFL ↗ · NFL ↗
F54 · MediumWeek 10 2026 was still in progress when the feed was captured: one of its fifteen games was final at retrieval on 2026-11-14. Any figure computed across the feed mixes one full week with one partial week, and the partial week will change without warning.The official Week 10 schedule page lists fourteen games still to be played at retrieval.Re-capture after the week completes and record the capture state on every derived figure, or restrict published measurements to completed weeks.Published finding · see Flagged for ReviewNFL ↗
F55 · CriticalThe price-snapshot store is empty, so closing-line value, the demotion gate's live triggers and any return figure are implemented but unevaluable. This is the operating consequence of F22 rather than a new discovery: the gates built this session cannot be run until a timestamped executable quote exists.Measured from the committed store at build time: 0 rows, 0 complete opening/closing pairs, 0% of the games in the results feed priced.A permissioned, timestamped executable quote archive with provenance, limits and settlement rules.
