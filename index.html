RESEARCH DESK / OVERVIEWAs of 14 Nov 2026 · UTC

Evidence first.
An edge must be earned.

An NFL line-origination lab for game singles, player props and joint bets, with the availability and roster-deficiency layer the earlier releases were missing. Built to test skill, not to reward a lucky streak.

Research framework · no validated strategies

No verified historical executable odds or point-in-time paired dataset has been obtained. Lines, measured correlations, test scores and rewards are intentionally blank. No wagering is enabled, and no manual input is required to read any page.
