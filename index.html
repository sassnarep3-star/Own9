RESEARCH DESK / OVERVIEWAs of 04 Nov 2026 · UTC

Evidence first.
An edge must be earned.

An NFL line-origination lab for game singles, player props and joint bets. Built to test skill—not reward a lucky streak.

Decision readiness, one row per requirement

Each number is computed at build time from a committed file. The gate column names what blocks the requirement; the last column names the measurable number that would clear it. Nothing here is a performance claim, and no requirement is marked met unless its test or record exists in the repository.
