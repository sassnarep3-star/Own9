Executive summary

A research workspace for originating NFL lines and testing whether strategies add predictive value—not a promise of profit. References are contextual unless explicitly described as evidence; proposals have not been independently validated.

Research status: not validated. No historical odds dataset, executable forecasts, backtest, correlation estimates, or live bets are available in this repository. No strategy has passed the gates.
