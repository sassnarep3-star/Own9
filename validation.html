Evidence gates

Proposed policy. Gates are intentionally strict and do not certify profit or eliminate randomness.

Correlation protocol

For each predeclared pair of binary leg outcomes, align by game and settlement, report sample size, Pearson/phi correlation, a game-clustered confidence interval and the observation window. Estimate joint win probability directly and validate it out of sample; a pairwise correlation alone does not price a multi-leg parlay. Do not substitute zero for missing estimates.
