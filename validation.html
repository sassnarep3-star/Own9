Measuring skill without pretending to remove luck

Report prediction quality and financial outcomes separately. A winning bet is not proof of skill; neither is a short positive-return run. Freeze the candidate family, compare matched targets, account for shared games/weeks and retain the untouched final test. The precise power study, resampling method and drift tolerances remain unimplemented and block eligibility.

Research, not betting advice. No verified edge or guaranteed return. Blank empirical values are unavailable and unverified, never zero.
