Validation · rules before rewards

Gates & demotion

Eleven validation gates decide whether a strategy may rank. Six triggers send a live strategy back to paper.

How demotion works
Demote to PAPER at the next evaluation. Re-entry needs a new registered version (G03) and a fresh forward window. Any trigger D1 to D5 demotes the strategy. A favourable staking result does not cancel a trigger, and no trigger can be overridden by ROI. No override. Any exception is logged in COORDINATION.md with its trigger ID and source links before it takes effect.

Validation gates G01 to G11

All gates are not evaluated. A strategy is eligible only after its signal gates G01 to G09 pass.
