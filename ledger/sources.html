Sources

Each official, market and method source, with the date it was read and what it does and does not show.

How sources are labelled
Official: the league, a government or the club. Market: a sportsbook page, used only for price observations, never called official. Method: a reference for a definition, never for a value. 'Retrieved' means read in this session; 'carried' means read in an earlier session and recorded in the repository.
