Executive summary

Bottom line. The official Week 10 data is captured and cross-checked. The model and strategy work is not done, because the verified inputs it needs are missing. Nothing here recommends a bet.

Key points

  • Official injury data captured: 160 rows across 14 Week 10 games from the NFL.com injury report. 66 Out, 10 Doubtful, 84 Questionable. Each row links to the report.
  • Schedule cross-check: all 14 matchups match the NFL.com Week 10 schedule. The 13 Sunday and Monday kickoff times and networks match it too. For the Thursday game, the schedule shows the final score (Commanders 24, Giants 21), and its kickoff and network come from the injury page only.
  • Depth gap: the NFL pages checked do not state starters or backups for teams. Starter/backup cells are blank. Only NFL.com-stated QB and WR replacements are shown (Starters page).
  • Injured-reserve scope is unclear: Lamar Jackson and Malik Willis are absent from the report, though headlines say they went on IR. This must be resolved before the report is treated as complete.
  • No line origination yet: no verified price source is in this dashboard. The NFL pages carry no betting lines. Zero strategies are tested or validated.
  • Singapore law: GRA states that only Singapore Pools is licensed for remote gambling, and that taking part in unlicensed remote gambling is an offence. Any use of this work for wagering is an owner decision that must comply with that law.
  • Repository: several sites contradict each other (gate counts, dates, duplicate data), and there is no licence file (Repo review).
