Q01Does the league publish an injury report at all, and in what form?Yes: a club-by-club report grouped by upcoming game with Player, Position, Injuries, Practice Status and Game Status, selectable by season and week.The report carries no publication timestamp per row.Answered · officialNFL ↗
Q02What do Questionable, Doubtful and Out actually mean?Uncertain, unlikely and will not play. Limited Participation is under 100 percent of normal repetitions; Full Participation is 100 percent.The league attaches no probability to the labels, so any play-probability number is a model output to be estimated, never a definition.Answered · officialNFL ↗
Q03Is a blank game-status cell the same as healthy?No. A player certain to play is deliberately left off the status report, and the policy instead requires an explanation if that player is later deactivated.The report alone cannot distinguish certain-to-play from unreported; the inactive list is the terminal record.Answered · officialNFL ↗ · NFL ↗
Q04How long does a reserve placement keep a player out?Under the 2022 rule change a designated player returns after four elapsed games, with eight returns permitted per club per season and at most two per player.Practice-window length, season-ending placement and playoff allocations are not in the text read.Answered · officialNFL ↗
Q05How do replacement players arrive, and how fast?Practice squads of 16, elevable to the game-day roster at most three times per player per season, plus trades, signings and waivers published as dated league transactions.Elevation counts are per player per season; a club’s remaining eligibility is not shown on the transaction rows.Answered · officialNFL ↗ · NFL ↗
Q06Does the league publish depth charts?No league depth chart was found: the club depth-chart path on the league site returns a 404 and team pages route the request to club websites.A dated negative observation of one path, not a proof of non-existence; the register carries it as F13, F17 and F19.Answered · checked absenceNFL ↗
Q07Can a depth ordering be obtained anyway?Only from club sites, and the one read states its chart is unofficial, written by that site’s writers and not necessarily reflecting the organization. It does provide first through fourth string columns.Opinion-based and unversioned: it can change without notice and cannot be reconstructed for past weeks.Answered · substitute sourceNew England Patriots (club site) ↗
Q08Is there an official snap-count or participation feed?No. The official player-statistics page exposes eleven counting categories and no playing-time or route-participation category.Per-play denominators therefore cannot be built from the official page, which blocks per-play replacement quality.Answered · checked absenceNFL ↗
Q09Does advanced on-field data exist at all?A Next Gen Stats section publishes metric explainers including coverage responsibility and expected points added.It is an editorial index. No dataset, field list, licence or historical coverage was verified, so it is not treated as an available input.Partly answeredNFL ↗
Q10Are wagering lines published by the league?No. No official league page publishes lines, so any line used here is a project output or a licensed third-party quote.This is the reason gate 1 has never passed and remains the single largest dependency in the project.Answered · structural gapNFL ↗ · Own9 repository (primary project record) ↗
