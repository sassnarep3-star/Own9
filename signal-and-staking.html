RESEARCH DESK / SIGNAL VS STAKINGAs of 14 Nov 2026 · UTC

Two boards.
One rank key.
Money cannot vote.

The leaderboard is split so that prediction quality and bankroll outcome are reported separately, with the rank computed only from the signal lower bound. The demotion gate sits on the same signal axis, so the thing that puts a strategy live and the thing that takes it out are measured in the same units.

Both boards are empty, and the emptiness is enforced

0 candidates are registered and 0 settled bets exist, so there is nothing to rank. That is not the interesting part: the interesting part is that a candidate submitting a staking run without a signal block is rejected rather than placed last, and a staking block cannot run at all without captured opening prices. A lucky run cannot mask a bad signal here because the two numbers never share a column, and the second one cannot be computed from thin air.
