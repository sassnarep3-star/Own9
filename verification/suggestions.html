Choose a path before building one.

Eight suggestions with cost, unblocked gate and a falsification test, so a reviewer can accept or reject each one on its merits rather than on its enthusiasm.

A suggestion is a proposal, never a finding

Each entry names the requirement it addresses, what it costs, what it unblocks, and what evidence would show the choice was wrong. None of them asserts a numerical NFL result, and none has been implemented by this session.
