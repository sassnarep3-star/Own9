What this section is, and what it corrected

An independent pass over the brief, line by line. It does not re-publish another session's numbers and it does not replace the site's canonical pages: every row links to an official publisher or to this repository, and the figures in prose are recomputed from the committed files at build time rather than trusted.

Correction, same session. The first draft of this section recommended building a scheduled multi-day capture of the NFL injury report. Reading NFL.com Terms section 1.3 (updated 16 May 2024) showed that recommendation to be wrong: systematic retrieval to build a compilation is prohibited without express prior written consent. The recommendation is withdrawn, the affected lines are now blocked on gate G-10, and the change is published here rather than quietly deleted — a reader who saw the first version is owed the reason.
