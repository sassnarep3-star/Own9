Washington sports-wagering law

Finding (verified)Source
Washington law authorizes sports wagering "on a very limited basis by restricting it to tribal casinos" (HB 2638, 2020).Washington State Gambling Commission - Sports wagering requirements and rules
Each tribal gaming operation must use a geofence so mobile sports wagers can only be placed while the patron is physically on the premises.Washington State Gambling Commission - Sports wagering requirements and rules
Prohibited participants include anyone under 18 years of age, agents or proxies, and athletes whose performance may affect the outcome.Washington State Gambling Commission - Sports wagering requirements and rules
Washington prohibits wagers on events where the outcome is already determined ("past postings").Washington State Gambling Commission - Sports wagering requirements and rules
Washington law has never allowed bracket pools, office sports pools, or fantasy sports.Washington State Gambling Commission - Sports wagering requirements and rules
Bookmaking (accepting bets as a business, charging a fee/vigorish) is illegal in Washington.Washington State Gambling Commission - Sports wagering requirements and rules
Sports wagering vendors and tribal operations are licensed/regulated through the Commission; full menu at the Commission hub.Washington State Gambling Commission - Sports wagering

Practical effect for a Seattle-area user: the strategies on this site cannot be placed through an online or out-of-state app from Washington under the rules above. Check current WSGC guidance before acting on anything here.
