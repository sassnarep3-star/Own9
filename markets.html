RESEARCH DESK / W10 SNAPSHOTSAs of 14 Nov 2026 · UTC

Every market we originate, opened and closed.

The price-snapshot store records opening and closing numbers for every market Own9 originates: the model’s fair line, the market line, and their difference as separate columns. Market columns are blank by policy — no official market price exists — and never inferred.

Model outputs, not market prices

The model_fair column is the deterministic output of the predeclared elo-v1 method computed from the verified official results feed. It is unvalidated and claims no edge; the market_line and diff columns are blank on every row because the NFL publishes no betting lines and no officially verifiable price archive was obtained (F01, F34). Acquiring that archive is suggestion S-17.
