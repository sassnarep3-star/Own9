Pricing discipline

For a no-push single with decimal odds D and model win probability p, expected net return per unit is p × D − 1. This is a mathematical proposal, not a verified market opportunity; pushes and voids require separate outcome probabilities. A fair no-push decimal price is 1/p. Margin, limits, availability and price movement matter.
