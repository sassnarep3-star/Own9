Why a brief instead of a research summary

A research summary records what was done. A brief records what is still open and what would end it. Without the second, an unknown can persist indefinitely because no row owns it. Every entry above names the artefact that would settle it, so the next action is visible without asking anyone.

Standing constraints

  • No wager is placed and no price is published as advice.
  • No numerical NFL claim exists unless an official or reference source is linked on the same row.
  • A blank cell is an unanswered question, never a zero and never an estimate.
  • Nothing becomes eligible by being written down; eligibility requires all declared gates.

Research, not betting advice. No verified edge or guaranteed return. Blank empirical values are unavailable and unverified, never zero.
