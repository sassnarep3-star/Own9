Injury & depth

Track injuries and the deficiencies created when 2nd, 3rd or 4th-string backups replace starters on offense and defense. Values are blank until point-in-time official data are captured.

i
No availability is inferred.

The NFL injury report separates Practice Status from Game Status, and the official roster exposes roster Status. A blank field is not "healthy". No historical as-of availability ledger has been captured, so every deficiency value stays unverified. Depth-chart tier ordering could not be confirmed from a live official URL and is flagged (F14).
