Suggestions

Improvement suggestions for better decision-making. Review each one, choose the best path, and only then implement. Nothing here is a decision already made.

i
A review queue, not an instruction list.

Each suggestion carries a rationale, the decision-making benefit it aims to create, an effort/risk note and a review status. A reviewer sets each row to Under review, Accepted or Rejected before any change is implemented. Source links describe the method or input; they do not endorse a suggestion.
