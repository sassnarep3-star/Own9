NFL / EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Executive summary

Status: not validated. No strategy passed every validation gate. Lines used for comparison come from an unverified third-party file. Nothing here is a betting recommendation; in Washington State sports wagering is restricted (see Flagged for review).

Summary

  1. What was built. A reproducible pipeline (tools/analyze.py) that reads a committed snapshot of game results and betting-line columns, builds a walk-forward Elo own line (no future information), and evaluates 3 single-bet thresholds plus one 2-leg parlay.
  2. Own line vs market. Over 2,639 games in 2016–2025 the own line has a mean absolute error 10.42 points versus 9.79 for the market column. The gap is 0.63 points (95% CI 0.537 to 0.716). The simple model is worse than the market baseline.
  3. Singles. Primary strategy S2 (edge ≥ 2 points) won 50.4% of 1,633 test bets (CI 47.9% to 52.8%). The breakeven hit rate at -110 is 52.4%. Test ROI at the assumed price is -3.8%. p = 0.783, far above the Bonferroni threshold of 0.0125.
  4. Parlays. Two-leg parlays from S2 legs won 25.1% of pairs versus 25.4% if the legs were independent (lift 0.987). The assumed-price ROI is -8.7%.
  5. Correlations. Same-week cross-game phi = -0.012 (CI includes zero). Same-game home-cover vs over phi = 0.024 (approx. ±0.039), so the measurable dependence is very small. Correlation is measured, not priced.
  6. Forward check (2026). S2 went 33-24 (57.9%, n = 59, p = 0.289). Too few games to conclude anything; it was not used for any decision.
  7. Luck. Randomness cannot be removed from single-game outcomes. The pipeline uses out-of-sample tests, a family-wise correction and season-level stability to avoid mistaking noise for skill. Under these tests, no skill is detected.
  8. Player props. Not run. No official prop prices were verified and no player-level dataset was retrieved in this session.
  9. Legal. The Washington State Gambling Commission describes sports wagering as illegal outside authorized tribal casinos. Any use of these results for real-money wagering or any reward program needs legal review first.

Method in brief

  • Own line: walk-forward Elo, K = 20, home field 65 Elo, 25 Elo per point, one-third reversion to 1500 each season. Parameters fixed before the test period was viewed.
  • Periods: tuning 2006–2015, sealed test 2016–2025, forward check 2026. Thresholds 1, 2, 3 points.
  • Pricing assumption: -110 on each side (not a verified sportsbook price). Breakeven 52.4%.
  • Source snapshot: nflverse nfldata games.csv, sha256 81a2786fd69546abbd4af6756100cfc6c12e5acbdbac884fe6f50054c02d222f (third-party; see Sources).

Limits

  • Betting lines have no documented origin, so market comparisons are unverified.
  • Only 5 game scores were checked against official NFL pages.
  • No executable, timestamped prices or limits were available, so economics cannot be validated.
