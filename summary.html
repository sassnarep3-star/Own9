Executive summary

Prepared 2026-11-04 · Data: nflverse/nfldata games snapshot (7,548 rows, 1999–2026) · Code: analysis/

1. What was asked

Originate our own NFL lines, research strategies (single bets, parlays, player-prop combinations), remove luck as much as possible, measure correlations, and rank strategies on a leaderboard with validation gates. This report covers single-game ATS, totals, rule-based situational strategies, and our own line model. Player props are not analyzed because no verified historical prop-line source was available (see Player props).

2. Method (how luck is controlled)

  • Pre-specified strategies only: 15 rules are fixed in code and were not tuned after seeing validation results. Totals use the pre-game line, never the final total.
  • Discovery vs validation: 1999–2015 is used for a stability check; 2016–2025 is the out-of-sample window where gates are judged; 2026 completed games are a forward window (reported, not gated).
  • Gates: G1 at least 150 bets · G2 Wilson 95% lower bound above the 52.38% break-even at -110 · G3 one-sided binomial p below the Bonferroni threshold (0.0033) · G4 same direction in discovery and both validation halves · G5 positive ROI in validation.
  • Our own line model: time-decayed ridge team ratings with home-field, walk-forward (each game priced only with earlier games).

3. Results

  • Strategies passing all gates: 0 of 15. Leaderboard ranking puts gate-passing strategies first, then validation ROI. Because none qualify, no strategy earns a reward under the stated rule.
  • Top three by validation ROI (none pass):
  • S04 Home big underdog ATS (spread <= -7): validation n=220, win 55.0%, ROI 5.0%, p=0.239; fails gates G2, G3, G4.
  • M03 Model edge >= 4 pts (either side): validation n=931, win 51.8%, ROI -1.2%, p=0.657; fails gates G2, G3.
  • S11 Divisional games: bet the underdog: validation n=958, win 51.7%, ROI -1.4%, p=0.682; fails gates G2, G3.
  • Own model vs market: mean absolute error over 2,761 games (2016–2025) is 10.38 points for our model and 9.78 for the closing spread. The market was more accurate.
  • Correlations: home covering and home winning are strongly correlated (r = 0.67, 95% CI 0.66 to 0.69). Two legs from the same game are not independent: in the sample, "home covers AND home wins" happens 44.1% of the time versus 27.4% if the legs were independent. Parlay math that assumes independence therefore misprices correlated legs.
  • Week 9 (2026) own lines: 15 games priced. The largest disagreement with the market is JAX at BAL (+10.9 points). That line is flagged as an outlier for review, not as a recommendation.

5. Limits and honesty notes

  • Historical betting lines are not published by the NFL, so spreads, totals, and moneylines cannot be confirmed from an official source. They are labelled Unverified everywhere.
  • Official verification coverage is small: 2 completed game scores were checked against nfl.com, and all 15 Week 9 (2026) games were checked against the nfl.com schedule (14 match; 1 kickoff time differs and is flagged). The remaining rows are Unverified.
  • An early draft graded totals against final scores (a look-ahead bug). It was caught and fixed before any result above was produced.
  • A passing backtest would still need out-of-sample confirmation and real execution costs (limits, juice, and line movement) before it counted as an edge.
