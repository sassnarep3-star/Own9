Suggested decision sequence

  1. Set the source-permission policy first. Prefer a licensed feed or documented consent; stop short of systematic scraping otherwise. Keep the uncertainty visible.
  2. Pick the canonical publication and data registers. Set one canonical landing route, one requirement register, and one origination implementation. Record mappings before marking alternatives superseded.
  3. Refresh derived documentation automatically. Build page counts and status summaries from their machine-readable inputs; test that displayed values match.
  4. Continue with the lowest-risk model path. Keep singles-only shadow forecasts as the baseline. Keep parlays and prop combos ineligible until direct joint-evidence and settlement-rule gates pass.
  5. Do not promote a leaderboard by ROI. Require pre-registration and chronological holdouts, proper-score skill with uncertainty, benchmark controls, captured executable prices, and forward evaluation before rewards.

These are recommendations, not owner decisions. Existing suggestions and decision questions remain the review surfaces.
