ORIGINATION / EVIDENCE

Price & results feed

As of 14 Nov 2026 · UTC

A price-snapshot store with opening and closing fields, and a results feed that is checked against the official publisher before it is trusted.

The opening columns are empty on purpose.

A price-snapshot store that records an opening number it did not see is worse than one that records nothing. No source obtained for this project publishes an opening price, so all four opening fields are blank on all 13,341 rows and the gap is carried as flag OD-02. Until that changes, closing line value cannot be computed for any market this desk originates.
