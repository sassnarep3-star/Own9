COMPETITION / DEMOTION

Demotion gate

As of 14 Nov 2026 · UTC

The rule that sends a live strategy back to paper, the thresholds behind it, and what it would have done to this desk’s own strategies.

The gate fired on the first window, on all three singles.

Replayed over the walk-forward, the trailing 200-bet lower bound on log-loss gain was already negative at bet 200 — the end of the 2010 season for the side and the moneyline, and week 2 of 2011 for the total. A live deployment running this gate would have stopped staking before any of the later money numbers in this desk existed.
