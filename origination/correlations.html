As of 14 Nov 2026 · UTC

Dependence is measured, never assumed. Every value is published with the number of observations behind it, and a gate decides whether a joint price may exist at all.

What refused pricing looks like.

The dependence between the margin error and the total error is r = 0.0325 on n = 4,296, with a 95% interval of 0.0026 to 0.0623. That interval straddles the declared deadband of ±0.05, so the gate refuses the price: 292 same-game combinations were offered and 0 were priced. The desk does not round the interval down to zero in order to get a number it can bet.
