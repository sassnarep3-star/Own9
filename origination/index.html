ORIGINATION DESK

Line origination desk

As of 14 Nov 2026 · UTC

Originate the number, prove it against the market, and publish the difference. Signal and money are scored apart so a lucky staking run cannot hide a bad signal.

Read this before any number on this site.

No strategy here has earned a stake. Every signal lower bound is negative, the desk’s own demotion gate would have demoted every live candidate on the first evaluable window, and the prices are recorded closing numbers from a file with no provenance or licence — not captured executable quotes. Nothing on this site is a recommendation to wager.

The other desk on this site

A second session built a market board on the main dashboard covering the same eight requirements: price snapshots, results feed, origination, signal and staking, replacement depth and correlations. It is not a duplicate of this one and the two do not conflict: that board works on the 2026 season only, with official weekly captures and a model fitted on 137 in-season games, and it has no recorded market prices to compare against. This desk works on 2010–2026, with 13,341 originated markets and a recorded closing price on every one, which is what makes the walk-forward, the backtest and the demotion replay possible. Read both; where they disagree, the difference is the input, not the arithmetic.
