ORIGINATION / LINES

Originated lines

As of 14 Nov 2026 · UTC

What this desk’s model says the line should be, what the recorded market line was, and the difference between them — published as three columns.

Three columns, never two.

The model’s fair line, the market’s line and the difference are separate columns on purpose. A single “edge” column hides which of the two numbers moved and lets a rounding difference pass as a view.

Origination by season

“Priced / settled” counts markets carrying a recorded price against markets with a settled result. The mean absolute difference is in the market’s own units: points for the side and the total, and probability points for the moneyline, because a moneyline has no line to differ from — only a price.
