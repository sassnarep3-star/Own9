ORIGINATION / METHOD

Method & constants

As of 14 Nov 2026 · UTC

The model, the order in which markets are originated, and every constant frozen before the results were looked at.

Why the order matters.

Originating the moneyline last forces it to inherit the margin distribution already used for the side. If the moneyline were fitted separately it could disagree with the side on the same game, and the desk would be publishing two different opinions about one match. The assertion is in the code, not in a comment.

The model

For every completed regular-season game in the training window, with h = home points and a = away points, the desk solves

h = mu + o(home) + d(away)   and   a = mu + o(away) + d(home)

for one intercept, 32 offence deviations and 32 defence deviations, subject to both deviation sets summing to zero. Predictions follow from the same fit: margin = (o_home − o_away) + (d_away − d_home) and total = 2·mu + o_home + o_away + d_home + d_away. Training residuals give the two spreads and their correlation. That is the whole model: no shrinkage constant, no learning rate, nothing tuned on the seasons being scored.

Published precision

Every derived number is frozen to nine significant digits before it is written to data/. The maths library is not bit-identical across CPython versions and C libraries — the error function behind the normal distribution can differ by one unit in the last place — and without a fixed precision the same inputs would produce different last digits on a different machine. Nine digits is far finer than anything this desk publishes: no page shows more than six decimal places, and the wider the interval the less the last digits can matter. The precision is frozen, not the drift: what it removes is noise below the resolution of any claim made here.

The engine uses one fixed-seed bootstrap (2,000 resamples over weeks) and no other randomness. It makes no network calls. Re-running origination/tools/run.py on the committed inputs reproduces every artefact byte for byte, and CI fails if it does not.
