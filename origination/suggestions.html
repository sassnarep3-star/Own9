How to choose

  1. Answer the questions on the question register first. A suggestion that does not close an open question is decoration.
  2. Reject anything with an empty “falsified by” column, whatever it costs.
  3. Prefer the path whose first item removes a critical flag on Flagged for review.
  4. Record the choice, then implement. Not the other way round.
