Suggestions

Each suggestion states what it unblocks, then offers two or three candidate paths with what each costs and what can go wrong. Nothing here has been implemented. The intent is that a reviewer picks a path before any code is written, because the paths are not equivalent and the choice is a policy choice, not a technical one.

S01 · Acquire a price source that carries opening numbers

Unblocks: The entire price-snapshot store. Without it, only a closing number can ever be recorded, and a store with one snapshot cannot measure whether a strategy beat a number that was actually available.

Evidence this session: No opening column exists in any committed file, and no official league page publishes betting prices.

PathLabelCostRisk / what can go wrongUnblocks
S01-aBuy a commercial historical odds feedMoney and a licence reviewLicence terms may forbid redistribution or derived publication; the repository would hold data it cannot publish.Opening and closing prices with timestamps, historically and prospectively.
S01-bCapture prices prospectively on a schedule from a public sourceSmall build plus a recurring jobBuilds history only from the day it starts, so years of backtest remain impossible; the source's terms must still be checked.A correct store from today forward, and a real archive in two or three seasons.
S01-cDrop the opening-number half of the requirement and record closes onlyNothingAny backtest silently assumes the closing price was obtainable, which it was not.Nothing new; it makes the limitation explicit rather than hidden.

Decision needed: Which path, and if S01-a or S01-b, who accepts the licence terms.

S02 · Write the snapshot schema before any price is stored

Unblocks: A store that can actually be audited. The schema must carry the market, the side, the number, the odds, the capture timestamp and the source, or no one can later prove which number was available when.

Evidence this session: The committed file has no timestamp column at all, so there is no way to tell when within the week any price was taken.

PathLabelCostRisk / what can go wrongUnblocks
S02-aOne row per market, side, snapshot timeSmallVerbose, but every value retrievable.Opening versus closing comparison, and any-time captures later.
S02-bOne row per market with opening and closing columnsSmallestCannot hold a third snapshot; forces an edit later.The literal requirement, no more.

Decision needed: Whether snapshots beyond opening and closing are ever wanted.

S03 · Settle the provenance and licence of the committed price file

Unblocks: Any use of the file outside the repository. Right now it can be measured but not safely built on.

Evidence this session: 7,548 rows, 46 columns, 1999 to 2026. No origin, retrieval date or rights recorded by any session.

PathLabelCostRisk / what can go wrongUnblocks
S03-aRecord the source, retrieval date and licence in a file next to the dataTrivialNone.Informed use, and a reviewer can judge the rights question.
S03-bQuarantine the file until a rights decision is madeLoses the only price data in the repositoryRemoves 7,424 closing spreads and totals from use.Certainty, at the cost of the data.

Decision needed: Who decides, and by when.

S04 · Make the results feed current and keep it current

Unblocks: Backtests and ROI at all. The feed is currently two rounds behind the league and nothing notices.

Evidence this session: 16 of the 137 played 2026 games have no result, including one the league published two days before this review. This session supplied 13 of the 16 from the league's own pages; 3 remain blank.

PathLabelCostRisk / what can go wrongUnblocks
S04-aScheduled job that pulls finished games from the league schedule pagesSmall build, recurring jobGame-centre pages are unreliable (two of three returned 'Hidden Score'); the schedule pages and Game Book PDFs do publish scores as text, so the job must use those.An always-current feed from official sources.
S04-bManual weekly refresh with a completeness checkSmallest build, ongoing human timeFails the moment someone forgets.Currency while attention lasts.
S04-cDo nothing, and publish the staleness on the dashboardNothingAny leaderboard reports a season that is two rounds out of date.Honesty about the gap, nothing more.

Decision needed: Who owns the refresh, and whether the job may store league data.

S05 · Add a check that fails when a played game has no result

Unblocks: Turns a silent gap into a visible failure. This is the single cheapest change on this list.

Evidence this session: The gap existed for 16 games and no check in the repository caught it, because nothing compares played-but-unresulted rows against what the league has finished.

PathLabelCostRisk / what can go wrongUnblocks
S05-aCount games with a kickoff date in the past and no result; fail the build above zeroTrivialRequires a trusted kickoff clock; a game finished an hour ago would trip it until the feed catches up.A gap that can never again go unnoticed.

Decision needed: Tolerance window after kickoff before the check fires.

S06 · Enforce the origination order in code, not in documentation

Unblocks: The side-then-total-then-moneyline requirement becomes testable rather than aspirational.

Evidence this session: The committed data holds all three markets per game, so the order is enforceable; nothing in the repository enforces it.

PathLabelCostRisk / what can go wrongUnblocks
S06-aOne function per market, called in a fixed order, each returning before the next is given dataSmallNeeds a test that asserts the order, or it will drift.A gate that can prove the order was respected.
S06-bOne model emitting three numbers, with the later ones masked until the earlier ones are committedMediumHarder to attribute a failure to one market.A single model with enforced sequencing.

Decision needed: Separate models or one sequenced model.

S07 · Publish fair, market and difference as three columns, and let the blanks be blank

Unblocks: The column contract the requirement asks for, without fabricating the one value that does not exist yet.

Evidence this session: Market line available for 7,424 games. Fair line unavailable: no model has been fitted and validated. Difference therefore unavailable too. What can be published today is the market's own error, and this session does: n = 121 in 2026, mean absolute spread miss 9.57, mean absolute total miss 10.66.

PathLabelCostRisk / what can go wrongUnblocks
S07-aThree columns now, fair and difference empty until a model passes its gatesTrivialLooks incomplete on the page; that is the point.The contract, with the gap visible.
S07-bTwo columns now (market and market error), add fair and difference when a model existsTrivialReshapes the table later.The same contract with nothing empty.

Decision needed: Whether an empty column is acceptable on the published page.

S08 · Score signal and staking on separate, non-overlapping measures

Unblocks: A leaderboard where a lucky staking run cannot outrank a genuinely better signal.

Evidence this session: No scoring rule in the repository separates the two.

PathLabelCostRisk / what can go wrongUnblocks
S08-aSignal scored on a stake-free measure of the number-beating rate; staking scored separately on realised returnMediumTwo ranks invite an argument about which one wins a competition.Both, reported side by side with neither hidden.
S08-bFlat-stake leaderboard onlyTrivialCannot detect a strategy that only works with sizing; discards real information.A clean signal rank, and nothing else.
S08-cCombined rank with the two components shownMediumA great staking run can still mask a mediocre signal in the combined number.One ranking, with the composition visible.

Decision needed: Which rank decides the competition prize, if there is one.

S09 · Compute the sample size a leaderboard needs before ranking anything

Unblocks: Prevents a one-season leaderboard from crowning noise.

Evidence this session: 121 completed 2026 games are available with prices. Enough to describe the market; nowhere near enough to rank a strategy that bets a subset.

PathLabelCostRisk / what can go wrongUnblocks
S09-aPower calculation against the real sample size and the expected bet rate, published before the season startsSmallThe honest answer may be that a season is not enough, which is uncomfortable but true.A defensible minimum-bets bar for eligibility.

Decision needed: Accept the minimum the calculation produces, whatever it is.

S10 · Adopt the provenance scale as the official substitute for a depth tier

Unblocks: Turns an unmeetable requirement into a measurable one, using only documents the league publishes.

Evidence this session: The league publishes no depth chart; the club route returns the league's own 404. Consecutive Game Books do print starters, substitutions, did not play and not active. This session measured 8 replacements from Washington and New York across Weeks 9 and 10: 6 came from the substitution list, 2 from the inactive list.

PathLabelCostRisk / what can go wrongUnblocks
S10-aFour-tier provenance scale (starter, substitution, inactive, absent from the previous Game Book)Small, given a Game Book archiveIt measures where a player was listed, not how good he is; it must never be labelled a depth chart.An ordinal, officially sourced replacement depth for every starting-slot change.
S10-bKeep the requirement unmet and flag itNothingThe requirement stays open indefinitely.Nothing.

Decision needed: Whether an officially derived proxy is acceptable in place of the tier that was asked for.

S11 · Build the Game Book archive the provenance scale depends on

Unblocks: Makes S10 computable for more than the three games one person retrieves by hand.

Evidence this session: This session read three Game Books across two weeks for two clubs. The league publishes one per game, and the game-centre page for each game prints its PDF link.

PathLabelCostRisk / what can go wrongUnblocks
S11-aScheduled job that follows each game-centre page to its Game Book PDF and stores the PDF with its URL and retrieval dateMedium build, ongoing storageStorage grows; the league's copyright notice on each Game Book says any use other than assisting media coverage requires written permission, so a rights decision is needed before anything is published from them.A season-long, officially sourced availability archive.
S11-bStore only the four list extracts, not the PDFSmaller storageLoses the ability to re-check a transcription; this session's own transcription of three books produced 366 matching checks and no mismatches, but the extract cannot be re-audited later without the original.The archive at lower cost, with weaker auditability.

Decision needed: A rights decision on the Game Books, then scope: all 32 clubs or a subset.

S12 · Set the minimum n before any correlation is computed

Unblocks: Removes the temptation to publish a correlation off a handful of games.

Evidence this session: The only correlation this session will publish is n = 7,397 (closing spread against actual margin, r = 0.4252). The two replacement cases that look interesting are n = 2 and are not published as a correlation.

PathLabelCostRisk / what can go wrongUnblocks
S12-aPredeclare a minimum n and an interval width, and refuse to print a value that fails eitherTrivialMost published cells stay empty at first, which is the correct outcome.Correlations that mean something when they appear.
S12-bPublish every value with its n and let the reader judgeTrivialA small-n number in a table gets treated as a finding.Full transparency, weaker protection.

Decision needed: Threshold value, chosen now rather than after seeing results.

S13 · Define the demotion gate as two separate mechanisms

Unblocks: A gate that can actually be triggered and tested.

Evidence this session: No strategy has passed validation, so nothing is live and the gate has nothing to act on. That makes this the right moment to define it, before anyone is invested in an outcome.

PathLabelCostRisk / what can go wrongUnblocks
S13-aSplit into an integrity trigger (immediate, no statistics needed: look-ahead, missing snapshot, unlogged stake) and a performance trigger (frozen threshold and sample size, evaluated only at predeclared checkpoints)SmallA performance threshold chosen now may be wrong; freezing it prevents tuning to a result, which is the point.A gate that is fair and auditable.
S13-bSingle combined gateSmallestAn integrity breach would wait for a checkpoint.Simplicity, at the cost of acting late on real breaches.

Decision needed: Thresholds, whether demotion is automatic or reviewed, and whether a demoted strategy can be re-promoted.

S14 · Handle clubs that are absent from the weekly injury report explicitly

Unblocks: Stops an availability model from silently treating missing clubs as healthy.

Evidence this session: Denver, Chicago, Philadelphia and Tampa Bay have no table in the Week 10 report, and no reason is published.

PathLabelCostRisk / what can go wrongUnblocks
S14-aTreat an absent club as unknown, not as zero concerns, and surface the count on the dashboardTrivialNone.A model that cannot silently invent health.

S15 · Key players on a stable identifier, not on a surname string

Unblocks: Stops the same player being counted twice when the league's own documents spell him differently.

Evidence this session: The Week 9 Game Book prints 'FB 45 P.Richard'; the Week 10 Game Book prints 'FB 45 P.Ricard'. Same club, number and position.

S15-bFuzzy name match with a manual review queue for near-missesMediumNeeds a human; silently merges different players if the threshold is loose.Cross-club and cross-season identity.

Decision needed: Whether mid-season number reassignment is common enough to need handling.
