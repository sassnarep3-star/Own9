Depth of replacement

The NFL publishes no depth chart and no first/second/third/fourth-string tier; the club depth-chart route on nfl.com returns the league's own 404 page (checked 2026-11-14). Instead of guessing a tier, this measures how far down the official Week 9 Game Book a club had to reach to fill a Week 10 starting slot. Every tier below is derived only from lists the league itself prints, and the source document is named on every row.

8 replacements measured · T2 = 6 · T3 = 2

This is an ordinal provenance scale, not a coaching depth chart and not a talent rating. It says where the replacement was listed one week earlier; it does not say how good he is. It is measured on 8 replacements in one week for two clubs, so it is a demonstration of a measurable quantity, not a validated predictor.

A trap worth naming

Two of the 8 replacements came from the previous week's not-active list, and both produced immediately. That is n = 2. It is not a correlation, no conclusion is drawn from it, and no staking rule should be built on it. It is published with its n precisely so that it cannot be mistaken for a finding.
