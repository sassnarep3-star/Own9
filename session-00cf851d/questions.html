Q07R3answeredWhat does 'originate the side first, then the total, then the moneyline' actually require of the data?It requires the model to produce a side number before it is allowed to see any total, and a total before it is allowed to see any moneyline. The committed file supports the ordering because it holds all three markets per game, but it holds them as three finished columns with no record of the order in which anything was produced.Nothing in the data. The order has to be enforced in code, and no code in the repository enforces it.S06source
Q08R3open-decisionMust the three markets be produced by three separate models, or one model emitting in a fixed order?Both are implementable against the committed data. Q09R4answeredCan the model's fair line be published today?No. No model in this repository has been fitted and validated, so there is no fair line to print. This session publishes the column with an empty value rather than filling it.A model that has passed the validation gates.S07source
Q10R4answeredCan the market line and the difference be published today?The market line yes, from the committed closing prices. The difference no, because it is the fair line minus the market line and the fair line does not exist. Publishing a difference without a fair line would require inventing one.See Q09.S07source
Q11R4answeredWhat can be published in the difference column honestly, right now?The market's own error: actual result minus closing price, which needs no model. Across the 121 completed 2026 games the closing spread missed the margin by 9.57 points on average and the total by 10.66 points, with a signed bias of -1.33 points on the spread and +1.00 on the total. n = 121.Nothing. But note this is a description of the market, not a tradable edge: the closing price is not a price anyone could have taken.S07source
Q12R5open-decisionHow should a leaderboard score signal separately from staking?The requirement is clear in intent: a strategy that picks well and stakes badly must not outrank one that picks badly and stakes luckily. Q15R6answeredCan replacement depth be measured at all from official documents?Yes, as provenance rather than as a tier. Consecutive official Game Books print four lists: starters, substitutions, did not play and not active. A club that fills a starting slot from the previous week's substitution list has reached less far than one that fills it from the inactive list. This session measured eight replacements across Washington and New York from Week 9 to Week 10: six came from the substitution list and two from the inactive list.Nothing for the measurement. What is missing is any season-long archive of Game Books, so it cannot yet be computed for more than the games someone retrieves by hand.S10, S11source
Q16R6answeredDoes a deep replacement mean the replacement will not produce?Unknown, and the two available cases point the other way. Both of the Week 10 starters who had been on the previous week's inactive list produced immediately: one rushed 18 times for 78 yards and two touchdowns and caught 3 for 44; the other caught 2 passes for 27 yards. n = 2.Sample. Two observations are not a correlation and must not be used as one.S11source
