RESEARCH DESK / SIGNAL VS STAKINGAs of 14 Nov 2026 · UTC

A lucky staking run
cannot mask a bad signal.

Two disjoint sheets: the signal sheet scores what the model predicted against official results; the staking sheet describes how money would be planned. Neither can move the other, and every price-dependent staking figure is blank because no executable prices exist.

The separation rule

Signal metrics are computed from official results only (walk-forward: each week priced on strictly earlier verified finals). Staking metrics would need executable market prices to mean anything — ROI, CLV and exposure caps are blank, not zero. A staking plan cannot be promoted on the strength of a signal score, and a profitable staking run (once prices exist) cannot rescue a signal that fails. Live activation is additionally gated by the live-to-paper gate on Leaderboard (session feaa600b, PR #34): its operating parameters are unset, so every entry stays Paper.
