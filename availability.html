Who actually started,
not who was listed.

Official Game Book lineups compared across consecutive weeks, plus the official Week 10 injury report. Replacement depth tiers are blank because no depth chart could be obtained from the league site.

The request was second, third and fourth string

The NFL publishes a game-day starting lineup and a roster status code. This project could not obtain a depth chart from the league site — the club depth-chart route returns an NFL 404 — and found no starter flag per snap or any first/second/third/fourth-string designation. This page therefore reports the verified part — who started, who left the lineup and who entered — and leaves every depth tier blank rather than guessing.

The depth-tier column is populated with blanks on purpose. The NFL roster page publishes number, position, roster status, height, weight, age and college — and nothing that ranks a backup. No depth chart was obtainable from the league site. Filling this column would require inventing a field, so it stays empty and the limitation is published instead.
