RESEARCH DESK / REPLACEMENT DEPTHAs of 14 Nov 2026 · UTC

How far down the chart,\nnot just that someone is out.

Every ruled-out starter and backup matched to the column of an official club depth chart, so the depth of the replacement is a measured number rather than a fact of absence. Depth is club-published context, not a league record, and the page says so on every row.

Read this before the numbers

Each club titles its own chart "unofficial", so a measured depth is the club's published ordering on the day it was retrieved — it is not a certified game-day depth record and it is never a settlement input. Where a ruled-out player appears in no column of his own club's chart, the depth is left blank instead of being inferred from his position label.
