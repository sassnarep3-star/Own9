RESEARCH DESK / ORIGINATION ENGINEAs of 14 Nov 2026 · UTC

Side, then total,\nthen moneyline.

The store, the order and the three columns every originated market must publish. Measured contents are read from the committed files at build time; the store is empty because no executable price has been captured, and the page says so rather than filling it.

Fail closed by construction

An originated market publishes three separate numbers: the model's fair line, the market line actually captured, and the difference between them. Where no market price was captured, the market line and the difference stay blank. A blank is not zero, and the arithmetic here will not substitute one.

Price-snapshot store

Every market the project originates gets one row per book per market with both an opening and a closing number. A row with only one of the two is rejected: scoring an opening line against a closing line that was never captured is exactly how a backtest manufactures an edge. The store is committed as data/snapshots/price-snapshots.csv and is currently header-only.
