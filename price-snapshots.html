Open and close,
every market we originate.

The snapshot store: one open and one close record per originated market. Open records are captured at origination; Week 11 close records are pending by design and stay blank until the close policy runs.

Store policy

Every market the board originates gets exactly two snapshot rows: open, captured at origination (2026-11-14T22:30:00Z), and close, to be captured at the frozen close policy (re-price at kickoff minus 60 minutes). Week 11 close rows exist with blank prices because the close re-price has not run — recording a number now would fabricate a price that no process produced (flagged F30). Market-price cells are blank throughout: no official source publishes them. The store is append-only; a close row never overwrites its open row.
