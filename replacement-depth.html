RESEARCH DESK / REPLACEMENT DEPTHAs of 14 Nov 2026 · UTC

Not just who replaced,
how deep the drop went.

A structured register of starter replacements: the depth tier the replacement was published at, so a second-string drop and a fourth-string drop are never treated as the same event. Blank where no club depth chart was retrieved; never inferred.

Why depth travelled is the tracked field

The fact of a replacement is verifiable from the official injury report, Game Books and league news. What changes a unit’s expected performance is how far down the published order the replacement came from: a 2nd-string centre is a different deficiency from a 4th-string one. This register stores depth_travelled (1 = 2nd string, 2 = 3rd, 3 = 4th) as a first-class field for every replacement where an official club depth chart supports it, and leaves it blank where no chart was retrieved (WAS, MIA, BAL, GB) or where the two official sources conflict (DET safety, F14). The Washington centre row is the strongest evidence in the register: both the outgoing and incoming starts are printed in official Game Books, though its tier stays blank because no WAS chart exists on the league site (checked 404, F19 lineage).
