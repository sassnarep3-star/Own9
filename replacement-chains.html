RESEARCH DESK / REPLACEMENT CHAINSAs of 14 Nov 2026 · UTC

Not just who replaced — how deep.

For every Week 10 Out/Doubtful player, the depth of the player who replaces them, derived only where a club-published depth chart places both players. Chains are chart-relative: club charts are self-labelled unofficial and mostly undated, so tiers are published order, not certified game-day depth.

How a chain is derived

Replacement = the next player published after the injured player in their depth-chart slot row. Depth = that replacement’s ordinal among the players listed at the same position label on the chart (e.g. second player at RB = “2nd string (chart order)”). Where the injured player is absent from the chart, tied in a co-listed cell, or last in the slot, no chain is derived and the row is published under conflicts with the reason — never resolved by inference. Chart-relative labels are weaker than certified game-day depth (F13, F44).
